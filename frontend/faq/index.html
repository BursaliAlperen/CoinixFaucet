Frequently asked questions

Quick answers about the platform's reward tools, account access and current feature status.

How do I get started?

Open the CoinixFaucet homepage, create an account when registration is available, and review the available earning sections.

What is CNX?

CNX is the platform's internal reward unit. The current display convention is 10,000 CNX per $1 USD; this is not a market price or guaranteed redemption rate.

How often can I use the faucet?

The current faucet interface is designed with a 60-second cooldown. Claims require the account and backend services to be available.

How do offerwall rewards work?

Third-party providers track and verify their own offers. Credit timing and eligibility are controlled by each provider and may vary.

Are withdrawals instant?

Do not assume a withdrawal request is paid instantly. Check the transaction status and current payout integration status.

Is Mining World live?

The current page is a UI and economy preview. Real GPU inventory, block settlement and mining rewards are not active yet.
