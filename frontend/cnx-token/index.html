CNX ECONOMY

Meet CNX

CNX is the internal reward unit used by the CoinixFaucet interface for tracking platform rewards.

Displayed conversion convention

The current platform convention is 10,000 CNX = $1 USD for internal display calculations. This is an accounting convention, not a public-market quote, redemption guarantee or claim that CNX is independently tradable.

Decimals

The planned CNX precision is 6 decimal places.

How CNX is used

  • Track eligible faucet and activity rewards.
  • Display a user's platform reward balance.
  • Support future GPU and Mining World economy features once implemented and enabled.

Important limitations

CNX is not a deposit, investment or guaranteed source of income. Any conversion, withdrawal or redemption is subject to actual platform rules and enabled payout integrations.
