ABOUT THE PLATFORM

A clearer way to explore crypto rewards

CoinixFaucet brings reward activities, account tools and the CNX platform unit together in one accessible interface.

What is CoinixFaucet?

CoinixFaucet is a cryptocurrency rewards platform interface. Depending on availability and account eligibility, users may access faucet claims, partner offerwalls, paid-to-click activities and account tools.

Built around transparency

Reward rates, withdrawal eligibility and processing are subject to the current platform configuration and partner terms. A displayed balance or estimate is not a guaranteed cash value.

Platform features

  • CNX reward-unit balance and account overview.
  • Faucet, PTC and offerwall interfaces.
  • Referral and transaction-history tools.
  • A Mining World concept interface, with reward settlement features still under development.
